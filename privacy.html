Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal information Glow by Grace ("we", "us") collects when you use glowbygrace.vercel.app, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We handle your information in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

What we collect

  • Your Google account details — when you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture. We do not receive your Google password or access anything else in your Google account.
  • Delivery details — the phone number, address, city and state you enter at checkout, and any order note.
  • Order history — what you ordered, prices, delivery option, order status and payment reference.
  • Your bag — items you add to your bag are stored only in your own browser until you check out.

Payments

Payments are processed by Paystack. Your card, bank or USSD details go directly to Paystack and are never seen or stored by us. Paystack tells us whether a payment succeeded, the amount and the payment method used (for example "card"). See Paystack's privacy policy.

How we use your information

  • To process and deliver your orders and contact you about them (by email, phone or WhatsApp).
  • To send your order confirmation email and order updates.
  • To show you your orders and saved details when you sign in.
  • To keep the shop secure and prevent fraud.

We do not sell your information and we do not send marketing emails unless you ask us to.

Who we share it with

Only the services we need to run the shop:

  • Supabase — stores your account and order information securely.
  • Google — provides sign-in.
  • Paystack — processes payments.
  • Resend — delivers our order emails.
  • Vercel — hosts this website.
  • Delivery partners — receive your name, phone number and address so they can deliver your order.

Some of these providers store data outside Nigeria, with appropriate safeguards in place.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed to fulfil orders, handle returns and meet tax and accounting requirements. Saved delivery details stay in your account until you change or delete them.

Your rights

You can view and update your saved details at any time on your account page. You can also ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account. Contact us and we'll respond within 30 days. You can remove Glow by Grace's access to your Google account at any time from your Google account settings.

Cookies and storage

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser stores your bag and your sign-in session so the site works as you'd expect.
